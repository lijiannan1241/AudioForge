LoudPipe is a desktop audio batch processor and document-to-speech tool that runs on your own Windows PC.

  • We do not operate user accounts and never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.
  • Your audio files, documents, scanned pages and generated reports stay on your device. LoudPipe does not upload them.
  • The only feature that uses the internet is speech synthesis. When you convert text to speech, the text you asked to convert is sent to the speech provider you selected, so that audio can be generated and returned to you.

1. Summary

The four points above are the whole policy in brief. The sections below give the detail.

2. Who we are

"We" and "us" mean the developer of LoudPipe, publishing under the name nannan2008. We are an individual developer. We do not operate any server that receives your content.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No account, registration, or sign-in.
  • No name, postal address, phone number, or email address, unless you choose to email us.
  • No analytics, usage statistics, session recordings, or heat maps.
  • No advertising identifiers and no advertising SDKs.
  • No crash dumps or uploaded diagnostic data.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No access to your files other than the files you explicitly open or drop into the app.
  • We do not sell or rent data, because we do not hold any.

4. Processing that happens entirely on your device

The following run locally. No audio or document content leaves your computer:

  • Loudness normalization, true-peak limiting, noise reduction, de-essing, and the effect chain (compression, EQ, reverb, pitch and time changes, distortion).
  • Sample rate, bit depth and channel conversion; container remuxing.
  • Metadata and chapter writing (RIFF INFO, BWF bext, iXML, ID3v2.3, WAV smpl/cue, MP3 CHAP, Vorbis CHAPTER, QuickTime chapters).
  • Waveform and spectrum rendering, six-band energy analysis, and QA report generation.
  • Text extraction from txt, md, docx, pdf, epub, srt, html and rtf files.
  • OCR of scanned pages, performed by the OCR engine built into Windows or by a locally installed Tesseract binary. Page images are not transmitted anywhere.

5. The one network feature: speech synthesis

LoudPipe has no cloud service of its own. When you use document-to-speech, text is sent directly from your device to the provider selected in Settings.

5.1 Default provider: Microsoft online speech service

With the default engine, LoudPipe connects to the online speech service behind the Read Aloud feature of Microsoft Edge, using the open-source edge-tts client library. The following is transmitted: the text to be spoken, in sentence-sized chunks; the voice identifier; language; speaking rate; pitch; and volume settings. Synthesized audio is returned and saved locally. This traffic goes to Microsoft, not to us.

5.2 Optional third-party providers (you supply the key)

  • Microsoft Azure AI Speech, at the endpoint for the Azure region you configure.
  • OpenAI, at api.openai.com.
  • ElevenLabs, at api.elevenlabs.io.

If you select one of these, the same text and voice parameters are sent to that provider together with your API key.

5.3 Voice catalog

With the default engine, LoudPipe retrieves the list of available voices (about 318 entries containing name, locale and gender) from the same Microsoft online service. This request carries no user content. The resulting list is cached locally so it can be reused offline.

5.4 Network metadata

Any internet request necessarily reveals your IP address and standard protocol information, such as TLS and HTTP headers, to the provider receiving it. We do not receive, log or store that information, because the requests never pass through a server we control.

5.5 What is never transmitted

Your audio files; your source documents; page images; file names and folder paths; generated audio; generated reports; your settings file; and your favorites list.

6. What is stored on your device

LoudPipe writes only under %LOCALAPPDATA%\LoudPipe:

LocationContentNotes
settings.jsonInterface language, selected speech engine, concurrency and retry preferences, last output folder, favorite voices, and — if you enter them — API keys and region for Azure, OpenAI or ElevenLabs.Plain text on your own disk. Not encrypted, never uploaded by us.
cache\The voice catalog, previewed voice samples, and per-page OCR results.Regenerated automatically; safe to delete.
presets\The presets you save as JSON.—
plugins\Python effect plugins you add.—

Output audio, audiobooks and QA reports are written to whichever folder you choose, which can be any location your Windows account can access.

7. API keys

Keys you enter are stored only in the local settings.json and are used only to authenticate your own requests to the provider you selected. They are never sent to us. Anyone with access to your Windows user profile can read that file, so treat it like any other local credential: where the provider supports it, use a dedicated key with a spending limit, and revoke it at the provider if the machine is shared or lost.

8. Children

LoudPipe is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

9. Retention and deletion

We retain nothing, because nothing is sent to us. On your device you can:

  • delete output files you no longer need;
  • delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\LoudPipe to remove settings, keys, caches and presets;
  • uninstall the app, which removes the installed program but may leave the folder above in place for manual deletion.

Content you sent to a speech provider is governed by that provider's retention practices, and we cannot delete it on your behalf.

10. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, export or erase. The practical way to exercise these rights is to delete the local folder described in section 9. If you believe we hold data about you, write to cdljn2022@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

For content sent to a speech provider, please exercise your rights with that provider.

11. Security

LoudPipe does not transmit your files. Local data is protected by your Windows account permissions and, if you have enabled it, by disk encryption such as BitLocker. Requests to speech providers use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Third-party services

Your use of an external speech provider is also governed by that provider's terms and privacy statement, whether Microsoft, OpenAI or ElevenLabs. We encourage you to read it. LoudPipe bundles FFmpeg for local media processing; FFmpeg runs as a local process and makes no network requests on its own.

13. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what leaves your device, for example by adding an optional cloud feature, this policy will be updated before that version ships and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the release notes.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in LoudPipe: cdljn2022@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

五、唯一联网的功能：语音合成

LoudPipe 没有自建云服务。当你使用文档转语音时，文本会从你的设备直接发送到你在设置中选择的服务商。

5.1 默认服务商：微软在线语音服务

使用默认引擎时，LoudPipe 通过开源客户端库 edge-tts 连接微软 Edge「大声朗读」功能背后的在线语音服务。传输的内容包括：待朗读文本（按句切分）、语音标识、语言、语速、音调与音量设置。合成后的音频会返回并保存在本地。这些流量发往微软，不经过我们。

5.2 可选的第三方服务商（由你提供密钥）

  • Microsoft Azure AI Speech，端点为你在设置中配置的 Azure 区域。
  • OpenAI，端点为 api.openai.com。
  • ElevenLabs，端点为 api.elevenlabs.io。

若你选择其中之一，相同的文本与语音参数会连同你的 API 密钥一起发送给该服务商。

5.3 语音库

使用默认引擎时，LoudPipe 会从同一个微软在线服务获取可用语音列表（约 318 条，含名称、语言、性别）。该请求不含任何用户内容。列表会缓存在本地，以便离线复用。

5.4 网络元数据

任何互联网请求都必然向接收方暴露你的 IP 地址与标准协议信息（如 TLS 与 HTTP 头部）。我们不接收、不记录、不存储这些信息，因为请求不经过我们控制的任何服务器。

5.5 永不传输的内容

你的音频文件；源文档；页面图像；文件名与文件夹路径；生成的音频；生成的报告；你的设置文件；你的收藏列表。
